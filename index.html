About Major Roleplay

Welcome to Major Roleplay — where your story begins.

Major Roleplay is built around one simple idea: you should be able to create your own story.

A World Built Around You

Major Roleplay isn't just about playing a character — it's about becoming one.

Every decision you make can shape your experience. Meet new people, build friendships, make enemies, start businesses, join organizations, rise through the ranks, or create a reputation that follows you throughout the city.

The world keeps moving, and your actions have consequences.

Real Roleplay. Real Stories.

We believe the best roleplay comes from players creating stories together.

Our community is built around immersive interactions, meaningful character development, and an environment where every player can contribute to the world around them.

From high-speed pursuits and organized crime to everyday conversations and unexpected encounters, you never know what your next moment in Major Roleplay will look like.

Built for the Community

Major Roleplay is more than a server. It's a community of players, creators, staff, and roleplayers working together to build something bigger.

We're constantly improving the experience, introducing new features, expanding the world, and listening to the people who make Major Roleplay what it is — the players.

Your Story. Your Choice.

There is no single way to play Major Roleplay.

Become someone. Build something. Make a name for yourself.

The city is waiting.
